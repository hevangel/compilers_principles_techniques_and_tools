Editions and scope

These programs illustrate selected techniques using original code and sample inputs. Chapter numbers point to related reading. A dash means no direct reading is assigned.

  • 1977 Green. Principles of Compiler Design by Alfred V. Aho and Jeffrey D. Ullman. Open Library contents.
  • 1986 Red. Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools, first edition, by Alfred V. Aho, Ravi Sethi, and Jeffrey D. Ullman. 1986 contents.
  • 2006 Purple. Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools, second edition, by Alfred V. Aho, Monica S. Lam, Ravi Sethi, and Jeffrey D. Ullman. Pearson contents.

The coverage gaps include scanner and parser generators, production code generation, and several advanced optimizations and analyses.
